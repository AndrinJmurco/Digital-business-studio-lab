# Outreach drafts for creative partnerships
Verify creator's public professional contact path; keep platform terms, anti-spam rules and user's age/contract eligibility in mind. Do not obtain hidden contacts, automate mass DMs, impersonate creator/agency or misstate familiarity. Ask permission to share a short gameplan. Limit initial message to: specific public observation, clear beneficial hypothesis, one tangible example, request for opt-in, no aggressive scarcity.

Produce variants for email and platform DM in recipient's language, one short follow-up only where appropriate; include send condition and spacing recommendation. Draft counterparty objections respectfully; do not use emotional manipulation or unverified social proof. No contact sent, no account access, no invitations or deals accepted without explicit user approval. Stop when declined or opted out. Keep outcome notes in CRM only from actual responses; synthetic test responses labeled DEMO.
