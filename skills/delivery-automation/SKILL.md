# Lightweight digital delivery and aftercare
Specify file names/versions, license and permitted use, access mechanism, purchase or signup confirmation, preview and functioning downloadable assets, receipts and tax handling by platform, buyer onboarding, quick-start instructions, accessibility, support contact, refunds/chargebacks, bug reporting, privacy and update policy.

Dry-run the full free or simulated delivery path, including broken link, duplicate order, inaccessible file, dispute and cancel/revocation behavior. No fictitious payment transactions. Estimate minutes per order and total recurring maintenance, cap at confirmed weekly capacity including acquisition. Automate only with verified connected and authorized tools; no silent website publishing, account creation, outreach, or recurring tasks. Deliver a small readable checklist and acceptance tests with results and remaining unverified steps.
