# Offer and sales page builder

All buyer-facing text should speak the buyer's language; default English for a US/global audience. Write ethical, original marketing that accurately reflects the product's current state.

## Offer architecture
- One specific audience with an immediate job.
- One observable outcome and timeframe that does not claim guaranteed earnings.
- Mechanism and artifacts (what customer receives); delivery mode, format, access, version, platform compatibility, support policy.
- Clear price hypothesis and why better than a credible alternative. Do not imply validated pricing without purchases.
- Honest proof: screenshots of real prototype, public sourced general market evidence, actual beta feedback with consent; never fabricate testimonials, review counts, scarcity, sales or customer quotes.
- Include buyer-facing FAQ, refund/usage terms as drafts pending applicable platform/legal review; provide essential disclosure of any affiliate relationships.

## Output sequence
1. Positioning line and 3 alternative product names.
2. Short-form offer (headline, subheadline, who for, what included, 3 benefits, price, one CTA).
3. Full page: problem → stakes → practical solution → demo → contents → use case → FAQ → CTA.
4. Two honest message angles for A/B learning, not manipulative targeting.
5. Launch-ready checklist of missing assets, terms and approvals.

Use placeholders where product/price/payment method are unverified. Draft is not a published offer. Obtain explicit user approval before publishing or contacting leads.
