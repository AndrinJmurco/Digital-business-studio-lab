# Creator monetization gameplan
Use `creator-opportunity-scout` and `audience-demand-miner`; cite public URLs and dates. Write for the creator in their language and tone, not a generic hype pitch. This is a **proposal** not a guarantee. Required sections:
1. Observation: public profile, audience and clearly attributed signals, uncertainties.
2. Buyer job: one repeated measurable problem and likely payer; specific alternative.
3. Product thesis: name, short promise, original format, 3–5 milestones, sample deliverables, likely time to value.
4. Test before build: 7–14 day ethical audience survey/opt-in or willingness-to-pay offer; numerical pass/revise/stop, channel and explicit owner approval.
5. Price hypothesis: expected fees/refunds, sensitivity, no manufactured income claims. If modeling royalties, show hypothetical scenarios, not expected earnings.
6. Partnership role and effort: operator vs creator responsibilities, license/ownership approvals, confirmed weekly hours operator capacity, launch time.
7. Clear one-question call to action asking for permission to discuss; never claim agency, endorsement, results, or access to creator's account.

Output a polished 1-page copy/paste gameplan and a source ledger, and flag Unknowns. User must explicitly approve before the message is sent.
