# Creator partnership deal readiness (not legal advice)
No launch or commercial use of creator content without documented, explicit authorization. Clarify: legal names/entity capacity (do not collect sensitive identifiers in chat), authorized signers and whether adult/guardian participation is legally needed, IP ownership of pre-existing and newly created material, permitted name/image/voice use, confidential audience/customer data handling, access controls, permitted channels, no unauthorized scraping, review rights, accessibility, claims substantiation, prohibited content, refunds, platform terms, tax, chargebacks, customer support owner, and termination/license wind-down.

Define term, effective date, territories, scope, product price approval, net receipts formula (gross minus taxes collected, platform fees, refunds/chargebacks, permitted ad expenses only if agreed), payout frequency, audit rights, minimum commitments and decision rights. For 0–50 dollar budget avoid spending on commercial contracts without capacity. Separate signed agreement from informal positive reply; do not offer a legal conclusion without official source or professional review. Provide a checklist and questions to take to qualified counsel; do not draft exploitative or unenforceable agreements. Require consent before submissions or signing.
