# Independent digital product formula
Public sequence: https://www.monetise.com/archives/events/mmoc-1.0/offer. This is a functional original methodology, not their paid training.

1. DEVELOP: select one buyer-job; evidence check and product charter; minimum transformation in one page, calculator, workbook, checklist or mini workshop. Ensure original rights and adequate factual accuracy. Create sample and acceptance tests, not a huge ebook.
2. DISTRIBUTE: choose one accessible channel with existing buyer intent. Verify country and age/platform eligibility from primary documentation. First aim at 5–10 actual interest signals / first sales over time, not guaranteed. Never rely on a marketplace listing to prove sales.
3. DELIVER: prepare functional file, version, fulfillment instructions, access test, customer support/refund process. Test anonymously without placing a real payment or enrolling other people.
4. SCALE: only after verified paying customers; experiment with pricing, conversion, niche/format, repeated traffic, referrals and repeat offers. Improve contribution margin while honoring confirmed weekly capacity.

Throughout use evidence grade Verified/Estimate/Hypothesis/Unknown, label target income aspirational, show net revenue vs income and estimate real labor. Outputs: one offer, MVP, distribution test, operational checklist, next decision. No publishing or purchases without permission.
