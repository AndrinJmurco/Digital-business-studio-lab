# Public creator scouting
Work from specific niche and geography. Search public profiles and official sites. For each public creator, record URL, platform, topics, audience and engagement observation with date, educational expertise clues, audience questions, offer status, accessible contact route, partnership likelihood hypothesis, and sensitive/restricted sector exclusions. Do not infer personal addresses or private emails; avoid scraping prohibited content or circumvention.

## Score (0–5 each, weights out of 100)
Audience pain/pay signal 30; education/product fit 20; true engagement and trust 15; accessible permission-based contact 15; unmet offer niche 10; feasibility for operator's time/budget 10. 0 if legal/IP risks cannot be mitigated. All scores analyst judgments with evidence/date, not objective or guaranteed buyers. Unknown monetization => unknown, not 'no product'. Prefer 5 carefully qualified leads to 100 unvetted contacts.

## Qualification
Two independent paid-demand signals where possible; public follower counts and comments are not proof. Compare existing products, reviews, price and whether sales are observed. Evidence log: quote snippets <=25 words/source, links and dates. For feasibility confirmed weekly capacity, avoid creators expecting daily content, community moderation or bespoke coaching from operator. Output ranked leads and no-contact draft audit. Add to `assets/creator-pipeline.csv` or local CRM only when user requests / approves data entry, using publicly available professional details.
