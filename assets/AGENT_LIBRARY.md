# 54 original specialist marketing and business agents

This catalog is an independent set of prompt recipes based on ordinary business disciplines and public feature descriptions of AI marketing systems. **It is not a copy of GhostWriter OS agents or their prompts.** Each item is invoked through `ghostwriter-workbench` in ChatGPT. Agents are instructions, not independently hosted software or autonomous account access.

Common input contract: operator location/jurisdiction (if relevant), real buyer persona, product/offer, channel, supporting facts and URLs, desired CTA, constraints, budget, actual evidence, and approval status. Fill missing facts with clearly marked assumptions; do not manufacture proof. Agent output must include the named artifact, one confidence note, a safety/accuracy check, one metric to track, and its current status (DRAFT until approved).

## Prohibitions
No direct posting, mass outreach, buying ads, scraping private data, impersonation, copyrighted curriculum reproduction, made-up testimonials, false urgency, speculative 'guaranteed' revenue, or unauthorized use of creator IP. No access to GhostWriter OS itself.
