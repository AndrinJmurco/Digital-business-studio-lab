# Public two-track business implementation plan

**Status: Gather more evidence.** Neither track has verified buyers, sales, or creator permissions.

## Track A: original digital product
- Define one audience and costly repeated job.
- Research five comparable offerings, noting exact sources, dates, advertised prices and whether purchases are evidenced.
- Collect ten independent public buyer-problem observations.
- Build a small editable quote-and-margin calculator and check calculations.
- Design a permission-based buyer test with buyer qualification, target price, sample, spending limit and preset Pass / Revise / Stop thresholds.

## Track B: educational creator partnership
- Review five public educational creator profiles and repeatedly asked public questions.
- Compare their current product offerings; state gaps as hypotheses.
- Draft one original monetization gameplan, without contacting anyone.
- Model illustrative revenue shares, fees, refunds, time, rights and platform requirements.

Never claim outcomes or authorize outreach automatically; keep sensitive project data private.
